Methodology

My Approach to Data

Every data problem I touch follows the same mental model — clarity first, then depth.

01
Understand the Question First
Before touching any data, I ask: what decision does this analysis serve? Bad analysis often comes from answering the wrong question perfectly.
02
Clean Before You Explore
Messy data produces misleading insights. I spend serious time on data cleaning and validation — it's not glamorous, but it's where accuracy lives.
03
Let the Data Surprise You
I don't go looking for a specific answer. I explore with curiosity — the Inc5000 finding that revenue has near-zero correlation with growth came from not assuming the obvious.
04
Tell the Story Visually
A great insight buried in a table helps no one. I build dashboards and visualisations that make the "so what?" immediately obvious — even to non-technical stakeholders.
